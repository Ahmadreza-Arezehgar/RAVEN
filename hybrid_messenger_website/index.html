Frequently Asked

When you're offline, RAVEN uses Bluetooth Low Energy to find nearby devices running RAVEN. Your encrypted message hops from device to device until it reaches someone with internet connection, who then relays it to the cloud. The message continues until it reaches your recipient. All this happens automatically - you just hit send!

Absolutely. We use AES-256 encryption (the same as banks and governments) with end-to-end encryption. This means messages are encrypted on YOUR device and can ONLY be decrypted by the recipient. Not even RAVEN servers can read your messages. Each hop in the mesh network is also authenticated using HMAC to prevent tampering.

Each device can connect to others within about 100 meters (300 feet). But here's the magic: messages hop through multiple devices, so the effective range is virtually unlimited as long as there are other RAVEN users between you and your recipient. In dense areas like cities or events, messages travel very quickly.

RAVEN is 100% free. No subscriptions, no premium features locked behind paywalls. All features including mesh networking, AI assistant, and encrypted backups are available to everyone. We believe secure communication should be a right, not a privilege.

Currently, RAVEN is available for iOS (iPhone and iPad). Android support is coming soon! We also have a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works on any modern browser with Web Bluetooth support.
