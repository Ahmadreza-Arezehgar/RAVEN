How Mesh Networking Works

📱 Friend

Messages hop device-to-device until they reach the destination

1

Send Your Message

Your encrypted message is broadcast to nearby RAVEN devices using Bluetooth and WiFi Direct.

2

Message Hops

Each device stores and forwards your message to other nearby devices, creating a relay chain.

3

Delivery Confirmed

When the recipient's device receives the message, it's decrypted with their private key.

4

Acknowledgment

A delivery confirmation travels back through the mesh to notify you.
