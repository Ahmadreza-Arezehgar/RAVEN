- use a RAVEN logo, app icon, or confusingly similar mark as your product icon

- imply that a fork, relay, store node, bootstrap operator, or hosted service is
  official, certified, sponsored, or endorsed by RAVEN.

Unmodified redistribution may describe itself as the official RAVEN software
only when the distributed bytes come from an official release and all original
notices remain intact. Operators of interoperable infrastructure should use
their own name and make clear that infrastructure is independently operated.

No statement here limits rights that applicable trademark law already permits.
No mark is represented as registered unless it is actually registered in the
relevant jurisdiction. Contact the repository owner for branding permission.
