How It Works

How the hybrid delivery works

You don't need to do anything. Raven intelligently routes every message through the best available path.

1
Internet

Online First

If internet is available, messages go through the server immediately. Fast, seamless, secure.

2
Direct Mesh

Nearby Devices

If you're nearby, devices can deliver directly via Bluetooth — no internet needed.

3
Bridge

Store & Forward

If there's no internet, nearby devices carry the message until one can deliver it — or until it reaches the recipient.

Text-only over mesh for reliability.

Mesh Posts

Posts that don't die

Spread locally, sync globally

When offline, text posts with hashtags can spread locally via mesh — then sync to the server when any device reconnects.

In low-connectivity areas — during travel, commute, or outages — your posts don't die. They travel through nearby devices until they reach the internet.
